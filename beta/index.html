Join the Tot beta

Tot isn't publicly available on the App Store or Google Play just yet. We're testing it with a small group of families first, and we'd love you to be one of them.

Testers get the full app, free, before anyone else. All we ask is that you use it at home and tell us what works and what doesn't.

How to join

Email hello@totcho.app and we'll get you set up. Please confirm:

  • whether you use an iPhone or Android phone
  • the email address for your Apple ID (iPhone) or Google Play account (Android), so we can send your invite there
Email us for iPhone Email us for Android

Or email us directly: hello@totcho.app

What happens next

We'll reply with an invite and simple setup steps. On iPhone, you'll install Tot through Apple's free TestFlight app. On Android, you'll get it through Google Play.

We'll only use your email to set you up as a tester and ask for your feedback.
