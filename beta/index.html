It's a beta. Please use Tot a few times a week over the next couple of weeks, and send feedback from inside the app, or email hello@totcho.app if you have any questions.

Join the iOS beta

Install Apple's free TestFlight app from the App Store.

Then tap Join the beta on your iPhone or iPad.

Tot installs through TestFlight. Updates show up there too.

Join the Android beta

  1. Join our tester group. Sign in with the Google account you use for the Play Store, then tap Join group.

    Join group

  2. Become a tester and install. Tap "Become a tester", then use the "Download it on Google Play" link on that page.

    Become a tester

If step 2 says the app isn't available, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.
